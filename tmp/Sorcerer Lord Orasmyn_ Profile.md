# Sorcerer Lord Orasmyn

**Legion:** Thousand Sons  
**Cult:** Athenean  
**Birth Planet:** Terra  
**Warband:** Scions of Erudition

# Physical Description:

Orasmyn is taller than most astartes, standing at 7 '11, though this may have been augmented through Tzeentch's gifts of mutation. His power armour is coloured in the blue and gold of the Thousand Sons, though with a slight metallic green tinge, as are the rest of the Scions of Erudition. He wears a high-crested helmet, which is completely featureless except for the eyes which burn with a bright purple fire, appearing to gaze directly into the soul… perhaps they actually are.

# Background:

Orasmyn is an extremely powerful sorcerer lord of the Thousand Sons, currently leading his warband, the Scions of Erudition, in the pursuit of any and all knowledge, especially that of a forbidden and dark nature.

A terran born marine, Orasmyn fought as a member of the Thousand Sons during the Great Crusade before the discovery of their Primarch, Magnus The Red. Proving an effective astartes, he rose through the ranks to become the chosen Lieutenant to Captain Ohrmuzd Ahriman, the two working side by side during many campaigns of the Great Crusade. The pair built up a strong friendship throughout these campaigns. He was present during the battle on Bezant where the flesh change first appeared, mutating his brothers so rapidly that their new, hideous forms ripped holes in their power armour as their mutated forms spilled out. This event had a profound effect on Orasmyn, leading him to intensely study the mutated corpses of his fallen brothers, especially their minds as this is where he believed the key lay. This quest for a cure became even more fanatical when his friend and captain Ohrmuzd would succumb to the change. Orazmyn had many sleepless nights, thinking himself on the precipice of a breakthrough that would never come. Upon Ohrmuzd’s death Orazmyn was promoted to the rank of Captain of Ohrmuzd’s company.

Upon reunion with his primarch, Orasmyn became a member of the Athenean cult, masters of telepathic communication and matters of the mind. He and his company were absorbed into the 1st fellowship, led by Ohrmuzd’s twin brother Ahzek Ahriman, where he served as the highest-ranking member of the Athenaeans in the fellowship,  and was at the forefront of the Thousand Sons actions during the Great Crusade. Orasmyn was even present at the Council of Nikea.  
The Emperors ruling against the use of psykers and the study of their abilities sowed the first seeds of anger in Orasmyn, as he saw the role of the Great Crusade as not only to reunify humanity, but to also re-learn what had been lost during the Age of Strife, and to gain new knowledge for the betterment of humanity. There was potential to steer humanity towards becoming a truly psychic race. The Edict of Nikea was a foolish, regressive, and counterproductive ruling in Orasmyn's mind.

During his time in the 1st Fellowship, Orasmyn had formed a good relationship with its Captain, Ahzek Ahriman. A relationship built on respect and admiration of each other's skills, Ahriman's in the ways of the Corvidae, and Orasmyn's in those of the Athenaean, as well as shared mourning for Ohrmuzd’s passing, so when the Wolves came to Prospero and Ahriman was the first to defy his Primarch and raise a defence, Orasmyn was right behind him. Feeling betrayed by an Imperium to which he had seen thousands of his brothers fight and die to forge from nothing, Orasmyn fought the dogs of Russ with a fury he didn't know himself capable of. He had nearly completely burnt out his mind, summoning oceans of warpfire and burning the minds of their assailants, when he and 1023 other Thousand Sons were teleported with Magnus to their new home in the warp.

On Sortiarius, Orasmyn mourned not just for his lost brothers, but for the vast collection of knowledge his legion had amassed. Possibly the greatest collection of knowledge and potential for learning that humanity had ever known was destroyed on Prospero that day. Orasmyn was ready to set out again to reclaim this lost knowledge, but he had a greater issue to tackle. The re-emergence of the flesh change rocked Orasmyn to his core. The memories of Bezant and of Ohrmuzd came flooding back, and he was wholly devoted to stopping the curse once and for all. Magnus's forbidding of any attempts to stop the change infuriated Orasmyn. He felt betrayed for a third time, first at Nikea, then at Prospero, and now his Primarch was condemning his brothers to excruciating pain and existence as little more than animals. When Ahriman came to Orasmyn, telling him of his plans to cure the flesh change; Orasmyn needed no convincing and was one of the first to join Ahriman's cabal.

When their spell was finally cast, Orasmyn was horrified with the result, reducing their once proud, noble, intelligent brothers to nothing but dust. When the cabal was confronted by Magnus, Orasmyn was aghast at what Ahriman had to say. He was right in that the flesh change was no more, but his claim that the Rubric was a complete success left him stunned. Is this what Ahriman had planned, did he not care that their brothers were no more? This left a scar on the relationship between the two.  
As a member of Ahriman’s cabal, Orasmyn was banished from the Sortiarius with the others who had played a part.

During his exile from the Planet of Sorcerers, Orasmyn gathered around him other exiled Thousand Sons, including many from his previous company, and set out to scour the galaxy for any hidden knowledge, seeking not only to enlighten themselves, but to hopefully find the reason the Rubric failed.

Despite the Rubric greatly enhancing Orasmyn's sorcerous powers in all forms, he remained dominant in matters of the mind, able to sense and maintain psychic links over great distances, as well as read the minds, emotions, and intentions of all those he laid his warp sensing eyes upon. As such, he can be a very charismatic person, reading the tone of a conversation expertly, saying exactly what the other person wants to hear so they can fall into Orasmyn's plans. Because of this he enjoys and respects others of strong character, and disdains those of blind obedience, as to be so is to surrender your own free will which he finds abhorrent. Note that trust and blind obedience are not the same, and those under Orasmyn in the Scions of Erudition respect and trust him deeply; a trust he has legitimately earned with each of them.

The Scions of Erudition now scour the galaxy seeking artefacts, tomes, and even people that can add to their already massive repository of knowledge, as well as to bring retribution to those who have wronged them and their legion. To this end they maintain vast networks of informants across the galaxy. A favoured way to encourage the accumulation of knowledge is for the Scions of Erudition (or those in service to them) to visit a planet and covertly spread a meme-virus that drives those infected to crave and collect any and all information and knowledge. They will then return years or decades later, after the virus has spread and its carriers collected vast amounts of information, to collect this knowledge. Uber Aemos, famed savant to Inquisitor Gregor Eisenhorn, was infected with his meme-virus due to the actions of the Scions of Erudition.

While they largely attempt to avoid the fighting between the many warbands of the traitor legions, the Scions of Erudition are regularly sought out by representatives of these factions who are looking to trade for information, especially when they look to make raids into Imperium space. The Scions of Erudition themselves have become somewhat of information brokers, usually trading for more information or psychically gifted recruits they can train, though they have been known to accept other things as payment.

Orasmyn was one of the exiled Thousand Sons to answer Ahriman’s call to Prospero in M32 to defend him and his Prodigal Sons from the Inquisition while they enacted a ritual that would teleport them to Sortiarius. This became the Third Battle of Prospero, where Orasmyn commanded his fleet to fight alongside many of his former brothers, including Iskander Khayon of the Black Legion, successfully driving back the Inquisition and allowing the Prodigal Sons to reach the Planet of the Sorcerers.

Intrigued by the reemergence of his former brother, Orasmyn sought out Ahriman after his attempted second rubric. The two met again for the first time since the Rubric on Ahriman’s ship “Word of Hermes” and immediately Orasmyn could sense the combination of grief that Ahriman carried for the result of the Rubric, and the drive to find a way to restore the Rubricae to their former selves. Though Ahriman had clearly gained a darker and more manipulative side to himself since the two had last met, Orasmyn saw in his brother a man renewed with purpose. A purpose Orasmyn was more than happy to assist with.  
While Orasmyn and his Scions of Erudition did not officially join Ahriman’s Prodigal Sons, the two warbands would work together on countless occasions in efforts and raids aimed at finding any hint or clue as to how to return their brothers, with the Scions of Erudition large stores of knowledge and vast networks of informants proving invaluable. Examples include the “Death of Dianaxis”, the raiding of the Harlequin library on Arcadia, and the harvesting of souls on Calliope.

# Resources:

As the leader of the Scions of Erudition warband, Orasmyn can call on all their resources as he sees fit. They are a modest size, numbering around 15 sorcerers, 4 of which form Orasmyn's inner circle and the command of the warband. Together they have about 107 Rubricae. They command a small fleet of mostly escort ships. At the head of their fleet are Orasmyn's personal Hades Class Heavy Cruiser Avesta, and the Murder Class Cruiser Mourning Prince.
